Get found by Indian universities hiring for leadership roles

Add your profile once. Universities and their authorized recruiters searching for Deans, Directors of Admissions, Registrars and other academic/administrative leaders can then reach you. You stay in control — withdraw anytime.

How it works & your rights

  • We store your profile only with your consent, recorded with a timestamp (India DPDP).
  • Your details are shared with universities/recruiters for hiring purposes only.
  • You can withdraw anytime — your contact details are then suppressed and no longer shared.
