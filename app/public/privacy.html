Privacy Notice

Last updated: [date] · Data Fiduciary: [Your legal entity name], [address], India.

1. What we collect

When you join the candidate registry, we collect the information you submit: name, email, phone number, city/country, current title and institution, years of experience, highest qualification, the roles you're open to, your LinkedIn/CV links, and your consent record (the exact consent text and a timestamp).

2. Why we collect it (purpose)

To list you in a registry that Indian universities and their authorized recruiters can search when hiring for academic and administrative roles, and to enable them to contact you about relevant opportunities. We do not use your data for any other purpose without your consent.

3. Lawful basis

We process your personal data only on the basis of your explicit consent, given at sign-up (DPDP Act 2023, s.6). You may withdraw consent at any time (see §6).

4. Who we share it with

Verified universities and recruiters who hold a paid account on our platform and search the registry for hiring purposes. We require them by contract to use your data only for recruitment and to honor your withdrawal. We do not sell your data for advertising or any unrelated purpose.

5. Retention

We keep your profile while it remains active and for [retention period, e.g. 12 months] after your last activity, after which it is deleted or anonymized — unless you withdraw sooner.

6. Your rights (DPDP)

  • Access & correction — request a copy or correction of your data.
  • Withdraw consent / erasure — at the registry page or by emailing us; your contact details are then suppressed and no longer shared.
  • Grievance redressal — contact our Grievance Officer below; you may also approach the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Nominate — nominate another individual to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity.

7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your data and will notify you and the Board of a personal-data breach as required by law.
